# Lumen Licensing

This directory contains the working licensing position and licensing-related commercial concepts for external Lumen distributions.

Licensing documentation records the intended boundaries, permissions, restrictions and entitlement models associated with different Lumen distributions.

These documents are **working product and licensing positions**. They are not themselves final legal licence agreements unless explicitly identified as such.

Exploratory documents in this directory preserve ideas for future consideration and should not be interpreted as adopted licensing or pricing policy.

## Current Documents

### Research Licensing

`RESEARCH_LICENSING.md`

Defines the developing licensing position for the external Lumen Research distribution, including:

- permitted research use;
- commercial-use restrictions;
- single-machine deployment;
- registration and direct distribution;
- Research distribution identity;
- redistribution restrictions;
- heartbeat requirements;
- privacy and retention principles; and
- matters requiring resolution before release.

The Research licence is the current licensing priority and is expected to establish mechanisms that can later inform commercial licensing.

### Commercial Licensing

`COMMERCIAL_LICENSING.md`

Preserves the current working direction for future Commercial licensing, including:

- commercial and production use;
- subscription licensing;
- distributed deployment;
- multi-user licensing concepts;
- licence and deployment identity;
- Aestimare and optional specialist Assessors/Periti; and
- commercial licensing questions deliberately left unresolved.

Commercial licensing is not yet finalised.

The document exists to preserve the current reasoning and direction without prematurely turning provisional decisions into commitments.

### Commercial Capability-Based Pricing Concept

`COMMERCIAL_CAPABILITY_BASED_PRICING_CONCEPT.md`

Preserves an exploratory commercial pricing concept based upon Lumen's compositional service architecture.

The concept considers:

- a minimum **Lumen Core** capability set;
- commercial licensing based upon the Lumen services actually required;
- incremental addition of Lumen services as requirements grow;
- service dependencies;
- Aestimare as an additional capability;
- Core assessment capabilities;
- separately available specialist Periti;
- potentially different commercial values for specialist Periti; and
- the possibility of commercial Lumen being economically composable in a similar manner to its technical architecture.

This document is intentionally exploratory.

It does **not** establish:

- commercial prices;
- a final pricing formula;
- a final definition of Lumen Core;
- implementation or migration mechanisms;
- final licensing units; or
- a commitment to capability-based pricing.

Its purpose is to preserve the reasoning behind the concept so that it can be reconsidered when commercial licensing becomes an active requirement.

## Document Status

The documents in this directory intentionally represent different levels of maturity.

**Research Licensing** is active licensing work required for the external Research distribution.

**Commercial Licensing** records the broader working position for a future commercial offering.

**Commercial Capability-Based Pricing Concept** records a specific exploratory idea that may later be adopted, changed, combined with another approach or discarded.

The existence of a concept in this directory should therefore not itself be interpreted as a licensing commitment.

## Principle

Licensing documentation describes **what a particular Lumen distribution permits** and preserves commercial licensing concepts relevant to those permissions.

Architectural and engineering work required to support those permissions belongs elsewhere in the repository.

In particular, future commercial deployment and multi-user architecture is documented under:

`development/future/`

This separation allows licensing, commercial thinking and engineering requirements to evolve independently without prematurely turning one into a commitment by another.
