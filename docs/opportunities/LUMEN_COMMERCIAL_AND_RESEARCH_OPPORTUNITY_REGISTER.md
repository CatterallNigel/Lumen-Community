# Lumen Commercial and Research Opportunity Register

## Purpose

This register records accelerators, funded pilots, research collaborations, grants, venture-client programmes, conferences, and other opportunities that may become valuable as Lumen and Aestimare mature.

An entry in this register is not a commitment to apply. Its purpose is to preserve the opportunity, record why it matters, and make the readiness gap explicit so that promising programmes are not rediscovered after their deadlines.

## Assessment fields

Each opportunity should record:

- organisation and programme;
- opportunity type;
- geographic and organisational eligibility;
- application cycle and important dates;
- commercial, funding, equity, and intellectual-property terms;
- required product and company maturity;
- relevance to Lumen, Aestimare, or a specific Peritus;
- suitable pilot proposition;
- current readiness and identified gaps;
- evidence or material required for an application;
- next review date;
- source links and date verified.

## Status definitions

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Monitor | Potentially relevant, but no immediate preparation is justified. |
| Prepare | Relevant opportunity with a plausible future application; readiness work should be tracked. |
| Ready to assess | Minimum eligibility appears satisfied and the current call should be evaluated. |
| Apply | A deliberate decision has been made to prepare or submit an application. |
| Not suitable | Reviewed and rejected, with the reason retained. |
| Closed | Application cycle or engagement has concluded. |

### Why it matters

BIND is more commercially relevant than a conventional mentoring-led accelerator. Its venture-client structure is intended to produce a paid deployment with an established industrial organisation without requiring an equity exchange. That could give Lumen an industrial validation environment, contract revenue, a credible reference customer, and behavioural evidence useful in developing Aestimare and specialised Periti.

### Candidate proposition

> Lumen creates and preserves operational evidence of AI behaviour. Aestimare evaluates that evidence to identify inconsistency, behavioural change and assurance risk across repeated industrial use.

A future proposal should lead with a bounded industrial assurance outcome rather than the complete Lumen architecture. Potential pilot outcomes include:

- reproducible evidence for investigating AI-assisted decisions;
- detection of behavioural change across repeated or longitudinal operation;
- provenance and replay for compliance or risk review;
- assurance reporting for an AI-enabled internal process;
- reduced manual verification through evidence-based, calibrated trust.

### Why not apply in 2026

The conceptual fit is credible, but the application deadline is too early for a strong submission. BIND expects a legally established startup, a deployable MVP and the capacity to define and deliver a substantial paid corporate pilot. Applying before those elements and the buyer-facing proposition are established would create delivery risk and present Lumen prematurely.

### Readiness gates for a future application

- [ ] Illuminates.One or the applicant business is legally established and eligible.
- [ ] Lumen has a stable, externally deployable release.
- [ ] A bounded Reasoning Assurance pilot can be demonstrated end to end.
- [ ] Aestimare has sufficient working capability to support the promised assessment outcome, or the pilot is deliberately scoped to Lumen evidence and replay.
- [ ] Security, privacy, data handling, deployment and IP boundaries are documented.
- [ ] A specific BIND use case and prospective venture clients have been selected.
- [ ] Pilot scope, delivery capacity, success measures and indicative commercial terms are credible.
- [ ] A concise product demonstration, pitch deck and founder video can be produced.
- [ ] Travel and programme participation can be accommodated.

### Review trigger

Review when either of the following occurs:

1. BIND announces its next international open call; or
2. Lumen reaches an externally deployable release and Aestimare can demonstrate at least one bounded assessment outcome.
