# Opportunities

This directory records external opportunities that may support the research, validation, adoption, or commercial development of Lumen and Aestimare.

Opportunities may include:

- startup accelerators and venture-client programmes;
- paid pilot and proof-of-concept programmes;
- research partnerships and independent validation opportunities;
- public grants and innovation funding;
- industry challenges and open-innovation programmes;
- conferences, showcases, and relevant calls for participation.

## Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to preserve potentially valuable opportunities until Lumen is ready to pursue them. Inclusion does not mean that an application has been approved or planned.

Each opportunity should be assessed against Lumen's actual maturity and strategic direction. A strong conceptual fit is not sufficient if the required company status, product readiness, security position, delivery capacity, or supporting evidence is not yet available.

The central register is:

- [Lumen Commercial and Research Opportunity Register](LUMEN_COMMERCIAL_AND_RESEARCH_OPPORTUNITY_REGISTER.md)

## Recording an opportunity

An entry should record, where available:

- the organisation and programme name;
- the type and purpose of the opportunity;
- geographic, organisational, and product eligibility;
- application dates and recurrence;
- funding, contract, equity, and intellectual-property terms;
- relevance to Lumen, Aestimare, or particular Periti;
- a plausible, bounded pilot or research proposition;
- current readiness and the reasons to apply, defer, or reject;
- the evidence and preparation required;
- a review date or specific review trigger;
- authoritative source links and the date on which they were verified.

## Opportunity status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Monitor | Potentially relevant, but no immediate preparation is justified. |
| Prepare | A plausible future opportunity with identifiable readiness work. |
| Ready to assess | Minimum eligibility appears satisfied and the current call warrants detailed evaluation. |
| Apply | A deliberate decision has been made to prepare or submit an application. |
| Not suitable | The opportunity has been reviewed and rejected; the reason is retained. |
| Closed | The application cycle or engagement has concluded. |

## Assessment principles

Opportunities should be selected because they can materially advance Lumen, not merely because they are available.

Preference should be given to opportunities that provide one or more of the following:

- independent research evidence or validation;
- access to a relevant deployment environment;
- a paid and properly bounded pilot;
- a credible reference organisation or research partner;
- evidence useful in developing Aestimare or specialised Periti;
- commercial learning without surrendering disproportionate equity, ownership, or control;
- direct contact with organisations that have a demonstrable Reasoning Assurance problem.

Applications should normally be deferred when participation would require claims that cannot yet be demonstrated, delivery capacity that does not yet exist, or a premature expansion of the product solely to fit the programme.

## Repository boundary

This directory records external opportunities and their strategic relevance. It does not define:

- the Lumen engineering roadmap;
- committed development requirements;
- licensing policy or adopted commercial terms;
- research findings or experimental evidence.

Those subjects remain in their respective documentation areas.
