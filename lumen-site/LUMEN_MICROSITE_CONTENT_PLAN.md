# Lumen Microsite Content Plan

**Status:** Planning  
**Version:** 1.0

---

# Site Structure

## Home

Introduce Lumen++ and the engineering philosophy.

**Audience:** Everyone

---

## The Problem

Explain why behavioural evidence, continuity and reproducibility matter.

**Audience:** Everyone

---

## The Lumen++ Ecosystem

High-level overview of:

- Lumen
- Trace
- Replay
- Assess
- Servire

**Audience:** Everyone

---

## Component Pages

Each component should include:

- Purpose
- Key capabilities
- Architecture summary
- Links to canonical documentation

---

## Research

Summaries of the research behind the platform with links to the full papers and notes.

---

## Engineering

Project timeline, selected engineering diary entries and architectural milestones.

---

## Documentation

Single entry point into the GitHub documentation organised by topic rather than filename.

---

## Demonstrations

Interactive demonstrations and recorded walkthroughs showing Replay, Trace, Assess and Servire.

---

## Community

Project news, repository links, discussions and ways to contribute.

---

# Content Philosophy

The microsite should summarise information.

Detailed engineering content remains in the GitHub repository and is referenced rather than duplicated.
